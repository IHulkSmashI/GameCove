**Fine without asking:**
- Saying your project works with GameCove, is based on GameCove, or is a fork of GameCove.
- Writing about, reviewing or making videos of GameCove.
- Packaging an unmodified official release for a distro under the GameCove name, as long as it's clear who packaged it.

**Please ask first:**
- Using the name or the logo for a modified version, a fork you distribute, or anything you sell.

A fork you distribute needs its own name and logo, so nobody mistakes it for the official GameCove. Official releases are the signed ones published at github.com/IHulkSmashI/GameCove.
