# Brand logos: not covered by GameCove's licence

The SVG files in this folder are logos of handheld makers, chip makers, game stores and launchers. They are
**not** licensed under GameCove's GPL-3.0-or-later and they are not GameCove's. Each belongs to its owner, as
recorded per file in [`app/data/brand_logos.json`](../../data/brand_logos.json) (`owner`, `source`, `license`,
`notice`):

- Most are unmodified Simple Icons paths (CC0-1.0 for the path data; the marks themselves remain their owners'
  trademarks).
- OneXPlayer is the maker's own SVG with its fills removed; AYANEO, AOKZOE, GPD, ZOTAC GAMING, Orange Pi and
  Amazon Games are faithful traces of the makers' official logos. These are the makers' trademarks.
- Heroic Games Launcher, Lutris and Bottles are those projects' icons under their GPL-3.0 licences.

GameCove shows them only to identify a device, chip, store or launcher (nominative use), smaller than its own
name, never as its own branding and never to suggest endorsement. GameCove is not affiliated with or endorsed
by any of these companies.

**Removal on request:** any owner who doesn't want its logo shown can ask (a GitHub issue titled
"Logo removal: <company>", or privately through the contact in [docs/BRANDS.md](../../../docs/BRANDS.md#logo-removal-requests)).
The logo is switched off at once in `brand_logos.json` and the file is removed in the next release.
